Neon Face Scan

Your face is tracked and drawn as a low-poly wireframe, and a second copy of the mesh floats beside you as a point cloud. Hold up fingers to change the colour. Open your palm to blow the point cloud apart. Everything runs on your device; no video leaves the browser.
